\title{\vspace{-2.5em}\bfseries Patent timing and industry ordering:\\ does patenting lead trademark vocabulary?}
\author{Eric Silver}\date{\today}\begin{document}\maketitle\vspace{-1.5em}
\noindent A patent is applied for before an invention is in public use; a trademark is filed when the firm is ready to sell. If $\dkl$ and patenting tracked one underlying event, patents should therefore \emph{lead} trademark language. Table~\ref{tab:timing} enters patents at four timings in the paper's within-firm specification. Table~\ref{tab:classes} orders the NICE classes by the lead coefficient.
