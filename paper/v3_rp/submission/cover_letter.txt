Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "Business Themes in the Trademark Record: Language Signals of Product Survival, Funding, and Listing," for publication in Research Policy.

The paper builds a filing-level text indicator on the complete USPTO trademark record - 13.99 million case files re-parsed into event-dated prosecution histories - separating how unusual a goods/services description is for its industry from whether its wording ran ahead of or behind the industry's own movement. It validates the indicator against the record's outcomes, principally the five-year proof of continued use, and documents two measurement hazards for trademark-text research: word-level scoring measures description length and drafting style, and pooling granted with abandoned filings mixes the examiner's selection with the market's. The full pipeline, corpus build, and an interactive per-class appendix are public at github.com/ericsilver/tm-vocabulary.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. A fuller working-paper version circulates as SSRN Working Paper 6954598.
