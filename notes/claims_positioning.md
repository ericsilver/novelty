## Disagrees with or qualifies

1. Murdock 2017 / Barron 2018 interpretation transfer: resonance = influence there;
   lead = failure here. Domain reversal; arithmetic portable, valence not.
2. Optimal distinctiveness at the market stage: interior optimum only at the examiner;
   the gate rewards atypicality monotonically.
3. Category-spanning penalty (Zuckerman/Hsu/Pontikes [?]): theme importers survive
   better raw; penalty only net of atypicality. Spanning protects through the channel
   the literature says is costly.
4. Trademarks-as-innovation-indicator at the firm level (Mendonca 2004 onward):
   sign depends on representation; proxy not portable to firm outcomes without the
   representation machinery.
5. (Pending wave_timing.py) If order within waves does not matter: disagrees with
   pioneer-advantage AND its critics, who dispute the sign but agree order matters.
6. Expert innovation lists (BCG/MIT): no robust alignment (already in paper).

## Novel findings

- Lead/atypicality rotation; lead robust across three vocabulary partitions.
- Era swing episodic, inside four tech classes, driven by theme composition of the
  leading fifth.
- Internet gap has one event-time shape on class-specific arrival clocks
  (machinery 2008 arrival peaking 2012-15; transport 1995 peaking 2000).
- Two costs, separable: crowded wave (indifferent to arrival order) vs steady
  within-theme earliness penalty.
- Conformed descriptions: refilers rewrite toward the class from both tails, whoever
  drafts; registered text is partly conformed (construct weakness).
- Global-vs-per-class atypicality: the gate protection of atypicality is mostly
  imported themes.
- Value concentration: 10 firms hold 18.5% of $19.2T linked peak revenue; their debut
  language ordinary-to-conventional.
- AI/blockchain debuts funded 4-5x the base rate (complements Castaldi et al. 2026
  AI-trademarks WP [+]).
- Pair lead costly (+2.3pp net) while word recombination protects (-2.8pp net);
  uncorrelated. Two kinds of combination, opposite signs.
