# Where to publish, and how this paper differs from what each venue prints

Assessed 2026-08-25, against the v3 draft: 55 pages, census-scale
administrative data, a new filing-level text measure, descriptive and
predictive claims with design-based estimates, no hypothesis apparatus, no
policy section, sections written to be read independently.

## Research Policy (the standing target)

Fit. RP is where the trademark-indicator conversation lives (Flikkema et
al. 2019; Castaldi's program; Seip et al. in the sibling Technovation).
Referees who know the data exist there. Measurement papers that introduce
an indicator and validate it against outcomes are a recognized RP genre,
and the five-year proof of continued use is a cleaner outcome than most of
that literature uses.

Divergence from the typical RP paper. (1) Length: RP articles run
8,000--12,000 words; v3 is roughly double that and would lose the waves
section or the success section to an online appendix. (2) Framing: RP
papers carry an explicit contributions-to-literature section and usually a
policy or managerial implications section; v3 deliberately has neither.
(3) Outcome: RP's trademark work treats filings as innovation indicators;
this paper's outcome is firm and product survival, which reads as strategy
rather than innovation studies. (4) The optimal-distinctiveness thread
(Zuckerman, Hsu, Zhao, Taeuscher--Rothe) is a management-journal
conversation RP readers will not all know.

## Management Science (Entrepreneurship & Innovation, or Business Strategy)

Fit. Large administrative corpora, novel measures, survival outcomes, and
estimates-first prose are all at home; long papers survive via
e-companions; the Fleming recombination and Golder--Tellis pioneering
threads are MS/marketing-adjacent. The paper's declarative style needs the
least rewriting here.

Divergence. MS expects a sharper identification discussion than the paper
offers --- the paper is explicit that lead is positional and predictive,
not causal, and MS referees will press exactly there. The two-audience
result (examiner vs market) would need to carry the theoretical weight
that a hypothesis section usually carries. Acceptance odds at MS are low
for sole-authored papers without a seminar circuit behind them, which is a
practical fact, not a quality judgment.

## Strategic Management Journal / Organization Science

Fit. The optimal-distinctiveness and categories literature the paper
speaks to most directly (Zhao et al. 2017 SMJ; Zuckerman AJS; Hsu ASQ;
Taeuscher--Rothe AMJ) publishes in these venues, and the paper's core
finding --- the audience that rewards conformity is the examiner, while
the market penalizes being early, at census scale --- is a direct
contribution to that conversation.

Divergence. These venues want theory built and hypotheses derived before
data appear; v3 is written in the opposite order and would need a genuine
front-half rewrite (roughly: optimal distinctiveness as the frame, lead as
temporal distinctiveness, the gates as audience evaluations). The
descriptive sections (waves, internet, surges) would compress to
robustness or move online. This is the largest rewrite of the options and
the largest prize among management venues.

## Strategy Science

Fit. Explicitly welcomes phenomenon-driven empirical work without
hypothesis apparatus, takes long papers, and its editors read the
categories literature. Of all venues listed, v3 could go here with the
least surgery.

Divergence. Smaller audience and citation reach than RP or SMJ; the
trademark-data community mostly does not read it.

## AER: Insights (a spin-off, not the paper)

Kelly et al. (2021) established text-measured innovation there. A 6,000
word version carrying only the construct and the five-year-proof penalty
would fit the format; nothing else survives the length limit. Worth
holding as an option if the main paper lands in a management venue and
the economics audience is still wanted.

## Recommendation

Primary: Research Policy, because the referee pool knows the data, the
measurement-paper genre fits, and the Castaldi correspondence is already
open. The cost is real: cut to ~12,000 words (waves and success sections
compress; the online appendix absorbs the rest), add a short positioning
section (the parked bibliography is the stock for it), and add two
paragraphs on what the indicator offers innovation measurement.

Alternative if the optimal-distinctiveness contribution should lead:
SMJ, accepting the front-half rewrite. Fallback with least surgery:
Strategy Science.
