Business Themes in the Trademark Record

Companion site for Business Themes in the Trademark Record: Language Signals of Product Survival, Funding, and Listing (SSRN 6954598), which scores the goods and services descriptions of USPTO trademark filings against the themes of the surrounding record and follows each registration through the five-year proof of continued use, SEC financial reporting, and IPO.

  • The paper (SSRN)
  • Online appendix — per-industry results, all 45 Nice classes
  • Theme explorer — browse the 500-theme model by Nice class
  • IPO viewer — registrations on the lead/atypicality plane, tiered by outcome
  • Code repository (GitHub)
